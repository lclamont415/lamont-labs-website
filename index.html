About

Lamont Labs is a consulting practice founded by Lindsay Lamont, focused on product advisory, go-to-market strategy, and operations for tech companies and mission-driven organizations.

Lindsay has built and led product and marketing teams across insights and analytics, fintech, and civic technology. That variety of experience drives how Lamont Labs works: cross-functional thinking, fast learning, and a bias towards action. She started Lamont Labs to bring those instincts to companies and organizations who need to move.

Whether you're finding product-market fit, scaling a platform, or mobilizing a movement, the work is the same: get the foundation right, move fast, and build systems that scale.
